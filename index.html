Application of BU100 Core Readings & Frameworks

Scout Mindset Julia Galef

Questioning Assumptions

Rather than adopting a "Soldier Mindset" blaming student apathy, our scout audit revealed students over-scooped because they feared long refill queue delays. This shaped our Fast-Track Refill Pass solution.

Factfulness Hans Rosling

Overcoming Negativity Instinct

Avoiding dramatic generalizations, we established baseline daily weighing (41.5 kg/meal). Hard empirical data proved small behavioral nudges yield a consistent 28.4% to 33.6% waste drop.

Superforecasting Philip Tetlock

Updating Priors with Micro-Pilots

We initially forecasted a modest 15% reduction. After running a 1-day table-card pilot, we updated our prior estimates to 30%, which matched our actual 33.6% outcome almost exactly.
